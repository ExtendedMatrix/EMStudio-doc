Mode
----

The mode, the node and the room are no longer a menu of the top bar: their one place is the section **Where you work** of the chip's menu, opened by the left label of the status bar (:kbd:`⇧⌘L`). The buttons below stay in the page, hidden, and that menu uses them; their labels and tooltips are these.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 24 20 56

   * - Button
     - When
     - What it does
   * - Standalone
     - —
     - Standalone: editing a local .em.json
   * - Sidecar (sync)
     - —
     - Sidecar: live-synced to a host (Blender/EMtools)
   * - Join Room
     - —
     - In a room on a StratiGraph node: the graph lives in the room, shared with its participants.
   * - Open this room in…
     - only in a room
     - Open the same room in the other editor — the graph is the room's, so nothing is transferred
   * - Share this room…
     - only in a room
     - Who is at this table, and whose the live copy is. Opens the node's panel for this room, in a new tab — that is where the ACL and the owner live, and where people, teams, invitations and the transfer are all in one place. EMStudio used to draw its own; it showed people but not teams, so it was retired.

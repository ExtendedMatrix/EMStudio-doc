Rooms
=====

Working online: signing in to a node, creating a room or bringing a study into one, entering it and sharing it, and opening it in the other editor.

A room is reached by other tools too, not only by the editors: whoever connects
another program to a room finds its requests in the `REST door of a room
<https://docs.stratigraph-eccch.eu/projects/node/en/latest/REST-API.html>`__, in the StratiGraph Server manual.
